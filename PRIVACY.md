# NaatBook Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 15 October 2026_

NaatBook is a personal notebook app for saving naats (lyrics, notes and audio). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

## Your data stays yours

- Everything you create in NaatBook — entries, text, folders, recordings and imported fonts — is stored **locally on your own device**.
- NaatBook has **no user accounts**, shows **no ads**, and includes **no analytics or tracking**.
- The developer **never receives, collects, sells or shares** your content or personal information.

## Optional Google Drive backup

NaatBook offers an optional backup feature that uses your own Google account:

- The feature works only after **you** choose to connect your Google account inside the app.
- Backups are saved only into **your own Google Drive**, in a visible folder named **"NaatBook Backup"**.
- The app can access **only the files it created itself** (Google Drive `drive.file` permission). It cannot see, read or change any other files in your Drive.
- Backup copies are transferred directly between the app and your Google Drive. They are **not** sent to the developer or to any other server.
- You can disconnect your account at any time from the app's Settings, and you can delete the backup files from your Drive at any time.

## Permissions

- **Internet** — used only for the optional Google Drive backup described above.
- **Microphone** — used only when you record audio inside the app, at your request.
